About

What drives me — and how I work.

I'm a student at School No.29, Sumgait with a strong interest in robotics, cybersecurity and applied AI. I enjoy taking an idea from sketch (Fusion 360 / FreeCAD) to a working prototype, and then documenting and sharing the process.

Competitions taught me to work under constraints, iterate quickly and learn from failures — whether it was RoboCross, WRO Virtual Games, SAF or Teknofest. I value teamwork, clear communication and reliable follow-through.

I also volunteer with Sumgait Youth House and Bir Könüllü, helping with events and peer trainings (e.g., "Data sphere job divisions", 2026).

What I'm like

  • Curious and disciplined — consistent practice over last-minute cramming.
  • Hands-on builder — CAD → 3D print → code → test.
  • Team player — experience in SUMracers and cross-school teams.
  • Communicative — comfortable presenting in Azerbaijani, English (B2) and Turkish.
